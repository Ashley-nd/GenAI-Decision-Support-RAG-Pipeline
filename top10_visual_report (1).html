2. Safety Risk Flags
  • The high incident counts at both crossings indicate significant safety concerns that warrant further investigation.

  • Crossing ID 870880G has a notably high fatality rate (9 fatalities), which raises alarms regarding safety measures in place.

  • The higher AADT at Crossing ID 163639F may increase collision risk due to greater exposure to vehicular traffic.

  • Substantial train movements at both crossings elevate collision risk, particularly in urban settings, where pedestrian and vehicle interactions are more frequent.

  • The injury counts at both crossings highlight the severity of incidents, necessitating a closer look at existing safety measures.

  • Gather additional information on local traffic patterns and pedestrian activity at both crossings to assess the context of incidents.
